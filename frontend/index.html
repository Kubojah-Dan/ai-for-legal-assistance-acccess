July 1, 2024 Criminal Law Transformation

Purana vs Naya Kanoon (IPC to BNS 2024 Matrix)

On July 1, 2024, IPC 1860 was replaced by Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), CrPC 1973 by BNSS, and IEA 1872 by BSA. Lookup section changes instantly.

Old: IPC Section 420 New: BNS Section 318(4)

Cheating & Financial Fraud

Applies to UPI scams, fake bank calls, non-delivery of paid goods. Punishment extends up to 7 years imprisonment plus fine.

Old: CrPC Section 154 New: BNSS Section 173(1)

Registration of Zero FIR Anywhere

Police CANNOT reject a complaint due to territorial jurisdiction. Mandatory Zero FIR registration with immediate transfer.

Old: IPC Section 302 New: BNS Section 103

Punishment for Murder

Death penalty or life imprisonment. Also includes specific provisions for mob lynching by 5 or more persons under BNS 103(2).

Old: IPC Section 498A New: BNS Section 85

Cruelty by Husband or Relatives

Protects married women against physical or mental harassment for dowry. Cognizable and non-bailable offense.
